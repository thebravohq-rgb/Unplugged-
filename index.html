FAQ

Before you start.

How long is the guide?

UNPLUGGED is a 50-page digital guide built around a 90-day operating system.

Why 90 days?

Ninety days is a practical operating period for sustained practice. The guide does not present it as a biological finish line.

What if I've tried quitting before?

The guide isn't built around simply trying harder. It focuses on triggers, access, environment, first compromises, urges, replacement, structure, and recovery.

What happens if I slip?

UNPLUGGED includes a 24-Hour Return designed to help you interrupt the spiral, identify what happened, repair a weak point, and return to the system.

Does UNPLUGGED guarantee results?

No. The guide makes no guarantee. It gives you a practical system to work, while the decisions and actions remain yours.

What happens after Day 90?

The guide includes a Day 91 Plan to move from program mode into sustainable practice, keeping the rules, protections, recovery response, and priorities that still serve you.

Is this professional treatment?

No. UNPLUGGED is a self-guided educational resource. The guide also explains when professional support may be appropriate.
